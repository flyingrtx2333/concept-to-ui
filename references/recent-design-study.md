## Observed patterns and transfer

| Reference / evidence | Visible design decision | Useful adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Paper Mono Font Site](https://recent.design/i/mi01tni-paper-mono-font-site), gallery detail preview | Oversized outline letterforms, visible curve control points and fine construction grid make the subject itself the hero; quiet off-white background and sparse small annotations | Build visual identity from the product's own subject. For an asset tool, carefully authored curves, time marks and numerals can provide character without extra slogans. Keep reading-size labels practical. |
| [WebGL Portfolio](https://recent.design/i/68a8v4h-webgl-portfolio-explorer), gallery detail preview | Black and white cassette objects overlap in depth; sharp highlights and camera crop unify the scene, while small peripheral type leaves the scene dominant | Use consistent lighting, depth and framing where an expressive scene fits. In a workspace, adapt those relationships to subtle layers and spatial transitions rather than introduce arbitrary 3D objects. |
| Sam Cheng personal-site thumbnail in the Web feed | A hanging identification card, photography and large serif name combine physical and typographic identity in one composition | Combine one meaningful object/image with carefully chosen type; avoid unrelated stock ornament and mandatory decorative English. Thumbnail observation only; full interaction was not inspected. |
| Liquid Glass hero thumbnail in the Web feed | Dark segmented surfaces and narrow highlights articulate edges around a compact text group | Material quality depends on edge, light and contrast relationships, not indiscriminate blur. Thumbnail observation only; shader/refraction behavior was not verified. |
| [Augen Pro](https://augen.pro/), original site, first frame and scroll | A large soft-lit head emerges from a white field; floating compact navigation and a small left text group balance it. The following pale-gray section separates a small left label from a larger statement, using blue selectively | Cohesive art and layout can make little text feel substantial. Use a meaningful focal subject, deliberate asymmetric balance and restrained accents. A financial workspace should derive its subject from financial/time information, not borrow the head imagery. Some initial controls were low-contrast during entry; do not copy that weakness. |

The gallery describes the cassette experiment as WebGL/Three.js and lists hover/microinteraction for Paper Mono. Those descriptions are discovery metadata: the study inspected preview frames, not original implementation or those interactions. Do not treat preview video as a verified hands-on prototype.

## Limitations

Direct navigation to several gallery detail URLs initially rendered only the shell; opening the Paper Mono and cassette entries from the loaded feed successfully revealed detail previews. Other selected overlays did not reliably open, so their observations remain thumbnail-only. Several lazy-loaded cards were blank; they were not assessed.

The original Shopify Design site exposed text but rendered blank captured frames in this environment; it is not a visual reference for this study. Avara stopped at a security-verification page and was not assessed. Do not bypass that check or infer its design from metadata.

## Decisions for future concept rounds

- Start from a product-derived subject and a distinct composition, then choose the material and motion language that supports it.
- Review visual craft through scale contrasts, balance, typographic detail, image/data authorship and coherent light. A tidy card grid is insufficient on its own.
- Offer contrasting art directions, for example editorial precision, atmospheric depth or a spatially continuous data surface, only where each fits the real task. These are possibilities, not fixed A/B/C presets.
- Keep copy short. Translate visual richness into composition, imagery, data graphics and motion, not subtitles, slogans or invented features.
- Reinspect relevant works for a new platform or aesthetic task; do not reduce this gallery to a permanent palette or effect checklist.
