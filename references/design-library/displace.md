## Observed craft

- A full-width cinematic scene sits behind a concise foreground text/action group.
- Compact solid navigation/search surfaces stay distinct from the varied background.
- The following white section contrasts large text with smaller modules and broad project imagery.

## Transfer

Separate expressive imagery from stable interaction surfaces. In H5, compact controls can float above an art region; in a tool, confine art to a suitable zone.

## Avoid

Do not transfer the site's claims, teaching copy, full-screen video or colorful module grid into a product that needs concise operational labels.

## Motion evidence

Pointer movement and scroll were exercised. Images are frames, not saved video or proof of responsive interaction; timing and autoplay behavior were not audited.
